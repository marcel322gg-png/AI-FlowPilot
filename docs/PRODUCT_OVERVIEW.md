# Product overview

FlowPilot is a local-first CRM demonstration for small teams that receive inquiries through multiple channels. It brings intake, duplicate awareness, AI-assisted qualification and manager review into one workspace.

## Workflow

- **Capture:** validate and persist an inquiry.
- **Qualify:** derive intent, summary, priority, score and a suggested response with local Ollama inference, or deterministic Demo Mode.
- **Review:** inspect the lead record, edit details, search/filter the inbox and follow its activity history.
- **Decide:** the manager chooses a status and next step. Suggested replies are not sent by the application.

## Included capabilities

Dashboard analytics with 7/30/90-day ranges; lead inbox and details; duplicate detection; status and record editing; activity history; service health; n8n qualification and error handling; optional synthetic sample data.

## Privacy and boundaries

The default Compose setup keeps services on the local machine and binds published ports to loopback. Database, workflow and model data persist in local volumes. Demo records are fictional. The repository does not claim a hosted deployment, customers, testimonials or measured business outcomes.
